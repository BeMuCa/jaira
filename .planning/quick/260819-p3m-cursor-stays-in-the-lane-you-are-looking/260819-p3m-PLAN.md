# Quick Task: the lane you are looking at stays the lane you are looking at

## The report

Open a ticket from the compact view (say one in Review), leave the ticket again,
then move that ticket to done from the CLI. The TUI swaps itself over to the
done lane. The user wants to stay on the page they were on.

## Established facts (read in the code this session — do not re-investigate)

- `reload()` (`internal/tui/model.go:200-223`) is called from the `changeMsg`
  and `tickMsg` handlers (`model.go:513-520`), so any external write — CLI,
  `git pull`, another session — runs it. It ends in `rebuild()`.
- `rebuild()` (`model.go:285-324`) remembers the selected ticket's ID before
  regrouping and calls `selectByID()` afterwards (`321-323`).
- `selectByID()` (`model.go:326-336`) sets **both** `m.laneIdx` and `m.cardIdx`.
  That is the bug: `m.laneIdx` is not only a cursor.
- In the compact view `m.laneIdx` **is** the focused step — `pipelineKey`
  (`pipeline.go:290-303`) moves nothing else, and `focusedStep`
  (`pipeline.go:281-286`) indexes the steps with it.
- In lane focus `m.laneIdx` **is** the lane filling the screen, and `m.cardIdx`
  is the cursor inside it.
- So when the selected ticket changes lane, `selectByID` drags the whole
  single-lane screen along with it. In the multi-column board the same line is
  the desired behaviour: the new lane is on screen already, and the cursor
  tracking your ticket is the point.
- `m.cols` comes from `Set.Columns` (`core/lane/lane.go:743-763`), which is
  `s.Lanes` in order plus any passthrough lanes appended. Indices `0..len(Lanes)-1`
  therefore line up between `m.cols` and `m.lanes.Lanes` — the compact view and
  the board agree on what `m.laneIdx` means. Lanes can still be added or removed
  between reloads, so the held lane must be remembered by **ID**, not index.
- Cards inside a lane are sorted by `UpdatedAt` descending (`model.go:306-313`),
  so an unrelated edit reorders them. Holding the lane must still follow the
  selected ticket *within* that lane, or the cursor drifts on every tick.
- `modeDetail` records where it was opened from in `m.detailFrom`
  (`model.go:891`), and defaults to `modeBoard` for callers that predate
  `modeLaneFocus` (`model.go:83`).
- `clampCursor()` (`model.go:338-356`) already bounds both indices, so keeping a
  stale `cardIdx` and clamping lands on whichever card slid into the gap.
- `applyMove()` (`model.go:944-965`) re-selects explicitly after its own reload
  (`m.selectByID(full.ID)`) — a move you made yourself still follows the ticket.
  Do not touch that.
- Test helpers: `newTestModel` (`render_test.go:75`), `laneIndex`
  (`lanefocus_test.go:21`), fixture has two backlog tickets ("Investigate flaky
  logout test", "Rate limit the login endpoint") — `render_test.go:62-63` — which
  is exactly what "the cursor falls to the next card in the lane" needs.
- Baseline: `go build ./...` and `go test ./internal/tui/ -race` green before the
  change (recorded in SUMMARY).

  transient, and `applyMove` re-selects deliberately.
- Behaviour in `modeBoard` must not change at all.
