# Teammates' waiting tickets in the band: folded line or hidden
Status: Proposed
Date: 2026-10-12

## Context
The band lists tickets waiting in a person's lane. On a shared board most of them may belong to teammates.
Two rules exist for the same case.

## Decision
Open. Side A (NZZDRH decision 2026-10-12, code today): own and unassigned tickets get buttons; teammates' fold into one line, Discuss only.
Side B (MG80DF, in signoff): band and counters show only tickets assigned to the person; others do not appear at all.

## Rejected
(none yet)

## Consequences
Side B removes the only place a person sees and discusses a teammate's open question from the cockpit.
