# What my weekly summary needs

<!--

- Target: Jira. Everything I copy goes into Jira fields, so write it in Jira
  wiki markup (h2., *, ||table||, {{code}} is not needed).
- Language: German, but without umlauts in the copy fields - our Jira mangles
  them.
- Period: Wednesday to Sunday, because I report on Monday.
- Bundling: at most two Jira tickets per week, by feature. Each gets a
  Summary, a Description (Worum es geht / Ergebnis / Noch offen) and one
  comment per day that names what was finished that day.
- Hours: split every day half and half between customer A and customer B and
  show both columns. Sessions end after 90 minutes without a commit.
- A day spent on acceptance in the browser has no commits; ask me for its
  hours instead of leaving it out.
- Internal ticket ids go at the end of each Description as a table: ID, title,
  state.
