- Target: our customer's ticket system. Everything I copy goes into its fields,
  so write it in that system's markup: headings as `h2.`, lists with `*`,
  tables with `||` header cells.
- Language: German.
- Period: Wednesday to Sunday, because I report on Monday.
- Bundling: one entry per customer ticket. Which internal tickets belong to
  which customer ticket is the milestone: milestone `proj-123` is ticket
  PROJ-123.
- Each entry gets a title, a description (what it is about / result / still
  open) and one comment per day it moved, naming what was finished that day.
- Hours: split every day half and half between customer A and customer B and
  show both columns. Sessions end after 90 minutes without a commit.
- A day spent on acceptance in the browser has no commits; ask me for its
  hours instead of leaving it out.
