- A lane you make with n in the lane settings now also lands in your catalogue ~/.jaira/lanes once the editor closes, so every other board offers it under '+'; an id already in the catalogue is left alone and the screen says the lane stayed on this board.
- Editing such a lane with E now saves the edit in your catalogue as well, as long as the catalogue copy was still the same file; a lane whose id is a built-in's (review, testing, ...) stays on its board: n does not copy it into the catalogue, and E does not change your catalogue override of that built-in.
- A new lane that still carries the template's id my-lane stays on its board only, even if you changed other lines; to get a lane into your catalogue, make it with n and give it an id of its own before you close the editor. When your editor exits with an error, the lane screen now says the catalogue was left as it was.
