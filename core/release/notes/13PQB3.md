- Read a review finding in the cockpit as it is laid out: press Read explanation and the Explain chat's note shows its finding, why it counts and one line per option.
- Answer an explanation in the cockpit itself: pick an option with the arrow keys and Enter, or Own answer… to type your own; the choice goes onto the ticket as a `decision:` note and is carried out at once — an option marked [give back], and any own answer, gives the work back with it as the reason; one marked [accept] accepts the ticket where Accept could; any other choice is sent into the conversation the band sits in.
- Pick an option in the explanation pane on the option itself: each is a numbered row with its whole text wrapped over as many lines as it needs, [give back] in red, [accept] in green and (recommended) highlighted, focused on the recommended one; press its number, or move the focus and press Enter; Own answer… takes the number after the last option.
- Press Verdict beside a waiting ticket to read the whole review verdict the band cuts off.
- Expect Explain on every reviewed ticket whose review gaps name something (anything but "none", alone or before a remark), not only where the verdict uses a word of reservation.
- Read decisions in the band as "decided": they now come from the explanation pane as well as from Discuss.
- Expect a `round:` field on a ticket once work is sent back to an earlier lane: it starts at 2 and grows by one with every send-back (parking in blocked does not count); a ticket without it is round 1, and you can edit it by hand.
- Expect `jaira set <id> review-verdict=...` to write `review-round:` beside the verdict, the round it judged; `jaira show --json` and `jaira list --json` carry both as `round` (1 when the field is absent) and `review_round` (0 when unrecorded).
- Expect Explain from review gaps only when the verdict is from the ticket's current round, and not when the gaps say "keine Lücken", "No gaps found" or "No defect found".
- Expect Explain only for a review verdict of the ticket's current round: a reservation in a verdict an earlier round left standing no longer raises it.
- Read an explanation you already answered with its decision under it after a restart of Claude Code: the cockpit reads "answered" off the ticket's notes (a `decision:` note after the `explain:` note) and no longer offers that choice again; a newer explanation is open again.
- Find Read explanation in the band for as long as a ticket carries an explanation, also once its verdict is an earlier round's; only Explain, which asks for a new one, waits for the current round's verdict.
