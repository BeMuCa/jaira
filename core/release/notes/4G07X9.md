- Pick a new board's lanes yourself with `jaira boards create <name> --lanes a,b,c --description "..."`, from today's board, your catalogue and the built-ins, instead of a board type.
- Find every board you create in your catalogue `~/.jaira/boards/` afterwards, and build it in any other repository with `jaira boards create <name> --type <board>`; `jaira boards types` now lists catalogue boards beside the shipped types.
- Share a board with the team with `jaira boards publish <name>` and commit `.jaira/shared/`; teammates list it with `jaira boards shared` and take it into their catalogue with `jaira boards adopt <path>`.
- Press B in the lane settings screen to create a board there: type a name and a description, tick the lanes, press enter.
