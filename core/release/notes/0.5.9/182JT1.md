- Close a finished epic with `jaira epic close <name>` (`--force` when tickets are still open) and take it back with `jaira epic reopen <name>`: it writes `status: closed` onto the epic's ref, `jaira epic ls` and the epic list in `jaira web` hide it, and `jaira epic ls --all` or the page's "Show closed" switch show it marked.
- Pass `--force` to `jaira epic add` to put a ticket into a closed epic: without it the command now exits 3, and `jaira web` asks before a drop onto a closed epic; the epic stays closed either way.
