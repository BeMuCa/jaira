- Give a milestone a one-line goal and a place in the layer with `jaira milestone create <name> --goal <text> --after <a,b>`; `--after ""` makes it the top of a swimlane of its own, and without `--after` it stays out of the layer.
- Change a milestone later with `jaira milestone set <name> --goal <text>`, `--after <a,b>` or `--no-layer` (which hangs what came after it on what came before it); an unknown predecessor exits 5 and an arrow that would close a loop exits 3 with the loop named.
- Read `goal` and `after` from `jaira milestone ls --json`: `after` is present only for a milestone placed in the layer, and `[]` means it is the top of its own swimlane; `jaira milestone ls` now prints the goal after the ticket count.
- Press `l` to open the links of a card or of an open ticket — it was `L`, which now opens the milestone layer — and close the link window with `l` or `esc`; on the board `h` and `l` no longer change lanes, the arrow keys `←` `→` do.
- Press `L` on the board to open the milestone layer: every milestone placed in it (`after:`) is a bubble filled as far as its tickets are done — the terminal lane, the logbook on any day, or archived from the terminal lane — with arrows from what comes first to what comes after; `L`, `esc` or `q` go back to the board.
- In the layer, move with the arrow keys; press `enter` on a `[+]` to create a milestone there (`n`) or connect one that exists (`c`), `e` to edit a milestone's goal and predecessors, `c` to give it the next colour no other milestone has, and `x` to take it out of the layer — its tickets stay and what came after it moves up to what came before it.
- Press `enter` on a bubble to see the milestone's goal and tickets: `enter` opens a ticket (`esc` comes back), `b` shows the board filtered to that milestone.
